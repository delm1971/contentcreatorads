Frequently asked questions

What is TikTok Ads Manager?

TikTok Ads Manager is TikTok's advertising platform for setting up, managing, measuring, and optimizing paid campaigns.

What is TikTok One?

TikTok One is TikTok's all-in-one creative platform. Current TikTok documentation describes it as a place for advertisers to find creators and production partners, create content, and manage creator-led campaign workflows.

Can creator content be used in TikTok advertising?

Yes, in supported workflows and with the proper creator authorization. TikTok One documentation describes options for syncing creator posts or ad creatives to TikTok Ads Manager for paid amplification.
