Learn how TikTok Creator Marketplace fits into TikTok One and how brands can approach creator discovery, collaboration, and paid amplification.

TikTok's current creator collaboration ecosystem is centered on TikTok One, its broader creative platform. For advertisers, creator partnerships can complement paid advertising by supplying native-feeling creative and authentic product storytelling.

Creator discovery

Brands can use TikTok One creator workflows to discover and evaluate creators for campaign needs.

Collaboration

Creator projects can involve invitations and applications. Brands should establish clear deliverables, usage expectations, timelines, and campaign goals.
